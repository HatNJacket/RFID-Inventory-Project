                      Audit sessions

                      A session bundles a scope — a rack of bins to walk-scan, or a slice of the 1-left queue — and tracks it across days and people until everything is accounted for. 1-left items tick themselves when their check clears; bins are ticked by whoever walks them.

                        1-left stock checks

                        The warehouse dashboard queues every product Shopify drops to 1 on-hand for a physical stock check. When shelf-counting work proves the stock exists — a bin walk-scan that heard the product's tag, or batch tagging that counted it, after the check was raised — the check clears itself from the dashboard (logged in History with the evidence, undoable with re-queue). A tag merely being paired doesn't count: that proves a box, not a checked shelf. If nothing is found the check stays, in case the box is somewhere weird. Nothing here ever changes a stock number, a bin or a barcode anywhere.

                          Recent 1-left actions

                            Audit a bin with a C72 sweep

                            Sweep the shelf on the C72 (SWEEP tab → SEND), then pull it here against a bin: every product the bin should hold, what answered, and any strays or unknown tags. The check itself changes nothing. Two buttons here do write, and both ask first: Set to N raises Shopify's on-hand for that one product (logged, with an Undo in History), and Record as batch tagged files the bin as done in this system only — it never touches Shopify.

                            Shopify vs RFID, by bin

                            Every bin scored by the sum of |Shopify on-hand − RFID units| across its products — the biggest total mismatch first, so received-but-missing stock floats to the top. Click a bin to see which products carry the difference. A sealed-case tag counts its full unit value.
